SMART LOAD CONTROL • NIGERIA FIRST

Smart outlets and switches that keep generators off longer.

The Problem: Homes in Lagos, Abuja, Benin City, and Port Harcourt that already have solar and batteries still run generators daily, because idle outlets and unmanaged appliances drain the battery overnight.

The Solution: Rookery is developing retrofit smart outlets and light switches that fit existing boxes and panels. A local first algorithm uses them to shut off idle loads and shift cooling to midday sun, so the home runs on solar and battery longer.

Status: The outlet prototype works and the light switch is in development. Our target is an 80% cut in generator runtime, and a field validation study is underway to measure it.

The Rookery Approach

Why solar alone can still leave the generator running.

Solar panels on a home built with local construction can still leave the battery drained by morning, because idle outlets and unmanaged appliances such as air conditioners and pumps keep drawing power. Rookery is building the control layer that manages those loads.

Where It Fits

Built for Nigeria first. Designed for other hot, outage-prone markets later.

Field Validation Study

How we will measure the 80% target

We have not yet measured an 80% reduction. The study installs prototype outlets and switches in real homes, records generator hours before and after, and reports the result whatever it shows.

  • ✓ Record a baseline of generator runtime, battery state, and standby load
  • ✓ Install Rookery outlets and switches and measure under the same conditions
  • ✓ Report the measured change in generator runtime and overnight battery life
Long-term vision

Where we are headed

Each Rookery home is the foundation for our longer-term goal: letting buildings trade electricity with one another, so a home with surplus solar can supply its neighbors. This is a long-term vision, not something we offer today.
